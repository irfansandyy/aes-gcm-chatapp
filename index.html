      HOW CIPHERROOM WORKS

      Understand what travels over the network.

      Manual cryptography, visible packet details, and two ways to connect.

      AES-256 in Galois/Counter Mode

      CTR encrypts data one block at a time. GHASH creates an authentication tag so modified ciphertext is rejected before it is shown. Each encryption uses a unique 96-bit IV.

      01

      Plaintext stays readable to you.

      Messages and keys use ASCII. Binary files are encoded into ASCII chunks before encryption. The inspector displays encrypted bytes as hexadecimal.

      02

      The secret stays in your browser.

      Shared-secret mode derives the same key on each device. A proof of key possession admits participants. A display name does not verify a real identity.

      03

      Files travel in small pieces.

      Chunked transfers use independent IVs and authenticated metadata. Files up to 1 GiB can be offered. Available memory, bandwidth, and a live sender limit the transfer.

      04

      History has a short lifetime.

      The relay forwards encrypted packets and keeps no conversation history. Messages displayed in your browser disappear when you leave or close it.

      YOUR TRANSPORT

      WebSocket room relay

      Every browser maintains a WebSocket connection to your server. The server delivers encrypted packets to other participants in the same room. Room names and connection metadata are visible to the server.
